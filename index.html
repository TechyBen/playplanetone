playplanetone

Planets and moons from a constructor (heat, activity, atmosphere, water, life). The constructor sets the order of geological steps, the steps build layers on the sphere (geodesic cells by default), and the layers paint the globe with pixel-art colour ramps. The level mix shows the percentages a level layout would take from this planet.
